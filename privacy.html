Privacy policy

In short: the Daily Tracker doesn't store anything you enter. Your answers stay in your browser.

1. Controller

Youth Growing Journey (YGJ)
Email: ygjcontact@gmail.com

2. Your entries

The Daily Tracker works without an account. Everything you enter (journal, gratitude, screen time, gym, meditation, plans and rating) is processed only locally in your browser. It is not sent to us or to anyone else and is not stored anywhere, not even in your browser's storage. When you close or reload the page, your entries are gone.

3. Opening the tracker in your browser

Browsers inside other apps (for example Instagram) block saving images. If you tap “Open in browser” or “Copy link” there, your current entries are put into the link after the # sign so they come with you to Safari or Chrome. This part of a link is never sent to any server, including GitHub's. The app you're in, your clipboard and your browser can still see the link, and your browser may keep it in its history. As soon as the tracker opens, we remove your entries from the address bar.

4. Image export

The export image is created directly in your browser. When you download it, it is saved only on your device. If you use “Share”, you pass the image on yourself to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies.

5. Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the site, GitHub processes the connection data this requires, in particular your IP address. According to GitHub, IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

6. No cookies, no tracking

We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising tools, or social media plugins. Fonts and logo are delivered together with the website. No content is loaded from third parties such as Google Fonts.

7. Link to Instagram

At the bottom of the page we link to our Instagram profile. Only when you click the link are you taken to Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.), where their privacy policy applies.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–18, 20 and 21 GDPR). Since we don't store any of your data ourselves, this mainly concerns the processing by GitHub for hosting. Just send us an email.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live.
